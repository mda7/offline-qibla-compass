How it works, privacy & credits

How it works

The app uses your phone's latitude and longitude to calculate the shortest-path direction to the Kaaba (21.4225° N, 39.8262° E). The bearing is measured clockwise from true north. On Android, the phone's compass and a local magnetic correction turn that bearing into the arrow you see.

Privacy and offline use

The APK calculates the direction on your phone. It has no Internet permission, account, analytics, or location database. It requests precise location to find your position. The HTML version uses your browser's location and sensor permissions.

Accuracy

Check the location shown above. Hold the phone flat and away from metal or magnets; these can affect the compass. The displayed location accuracy describes the position fix, not the compass accuracy.
