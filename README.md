# offline-qibla-compass

Find the direction to the Kaaba using your Android phone’s GPS and compass.
The app works offline and calculates the Qibla direction on your device.

## Privacy
Your location stays on your phone. The app requests precise location to calculate the Qibla bearing. It has no tracking, analytics, accounts, or internet permission.
